Frequently Asked Questions

Are the doctors qualified?

Yes, all our doctors are board-certified, state-licensed, and go through a rigorous background check and interview process.

How long is a typical consultation?

Most urgent care visits last between 10 to 15 minutes. Therapy and psychiatric sessions typically last 45 minutes.

Can I get a doctor's note for work?

Absolutely. If clinically appropriate, the doctor can provide a digital medical certificate for your employer or school.
