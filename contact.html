Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Most patients are able to connect with a primary care physician within 15 minutes. For specialists, you can usually book a consultation within 24 to 48 hours depending on their specific availability.

Are my medical records secure?

Yes, absolutely. We use bank-level encryption and are fully HIPAA compliant. Your medical records are strictly confidential and only accessible by you and your designated healthcare providers.

Do you accept my health insurance?

We accept most major insurance plans including Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, and Blue Cross Blue Shield. You can verify your specific coverage during the sign-up process before booking.

Can doctors prescribe medication online?

Yes, our certified doctors can write and refill prescriptions for non-controlled substances. The prescription will be sent electronically to your local pharmacy for easy pickup.

What if I need technical support during a call?

We have a 24/7 dedicated technical support team. If you experience video or audio issues, you can click the "Help" button in the consultation room to instantly connect with an agent.
